# Register

One entry per session that ran rada-anchor here (card 2.2; RULES.md, "REGISTER.md"). Append-only
(RA-11). The header feeds `rada-tribute`; values in angle brackets are placeholders, and a
placeholder counts as absent. Tribute, haiku and header texts are in the user's language.

- title: <optional: the page's title>
- language: <the user's language: ru or en; any other gets English labels>
- project: <project>
- epigraph: <optional: the user's words about the agents> — <who>
- opening: <captions: a place | a date and time>
- prologue: <a few sentences on how the agents came to work here>
- epilogue: <a few closing sentences>
- menu: <optional: the answers of the response menu, split by " | "; the cursor stops on the last>
- coda: <optional: the user's last word before the final line; paragraphs split by " | ">
- coda_by: <optional: who said it, and when>
- final: <optional: the last line; by default «Я вернусь.» or "I'll be back.">
- final_sub: <optional: a line under it>
- music: <optional: an https link, opened in a new tab>
- hud: <optional: one line for the corner of the screen>
